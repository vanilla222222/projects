Why rebuild rather than extend

Chemical Graph Constructor's planner is good at what it is for. Its limits are structural rather than incidental, and each one is the sort of thing that is easier to design out at the start than to retrofit.

Chemical Graph ConstructorHereWhy it matters
9 elements: C N O S P F Cl Br I 31, with valence lists No boron means no Suzuki coupling; no silicon means TBS and TMS are labels rather than chemistry. One valence per element makes every sulfone a permanent error.
maxDepth: 3, 1.5 s budget depth to 20, budget to 10 min Real routes run eight to fifteen steps. Three is a hint, not a route.
27 settings, one hand-written validator 31 settings, declared once There, adding a setting means editing the defaults, the validator, the bounds, the UI and the tests. Here the row generates all of them.
One priority choice Five independent weights Cost and precedent can be traded off against each other instead of one winning outright.
Stops at a depth limit Stops at a buyable catalogue A route should end where you can place an order, not where the counter ran out.

Ask it something

Three questions, not three settings of one — each takes different inputs and stops on a different condition, so the mode decides which fields below mean anything.

ModeYou give itIt answers
routea target How to make it, working backwards until everything is something you can order.
patha start and a target How to get from the one to the other. The route has to actually use the start rather than buying its way around it.
analoguesa target Close relatives of it, and how to make those — for when the question is "something like this, that I can actually synthesise".

Settings, five ways

Every control below is generated from the schema, so the typed line, the link, the JSON and the saved profile cannot drift apart from it. They compose in order — defaults, then a saved profile, then the URL, then what you type, then anything a single search overrides — so a shared link can carry a policy and one word can adjust it without disturbing the rest.

Built the way Chemical Graph Constructor is built: open the file, no build step. Nothing here is chemistry advice.
