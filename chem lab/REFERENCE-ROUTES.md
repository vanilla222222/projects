# Benign reference-route comparisons

The Path Finder reference panel replays declared intermediate/product edges against the current forward rules and workspace checks. It reports reproduced and unsupported edges separately. It does not measure blind route discovery, general accuracy, experimental yield or successful preparation.

The MiraCosta source uses hydrolysis followed by separate acidification, then acetylation of the isolated intermediate. The benchmark checks neutral product identities after work-up. It does not mix the source's sequential acid/base stages into a simultaneous reaction or simulate purification. The model may reproduce only part of this documented route; that is a coverage gap, not evidence against the source.

The other sequences are constructed exercises, not exact published preparative sequences. In particular, the ring-cleavage challenge is retained even when unsupported so that missing chemistry stays visible. Conditions in these records are model inputs, not complete laboratory procedures. Sources were checked on 2026-10-23. No source images, yields or procedure text are reproduced.
