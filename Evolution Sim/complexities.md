# Complexity Map

Scores: 1 = easy/simple, 3 = longer but easily expandable, 5 = complicated/needs a little thought, 7 = challenging to expand quickly, 10 = unique/needs creative thought. Initial sizing pass (skimmed, not deeply reviewed).

| File | Score | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `index.html` | 2 | Static markup: canvas, panels, ordered script tags. |
| `css/style.css` | 3.5 | Long but flat styling; no logic. All colors are tokens with a duplicated light set (media query plus `[data-theme='light']`), which must be kept in sync. |
| `js/noise.js` | 2 | SeededRandom and PerlinNoise classes, standard algorithms. |
| `js/biomes.js` | 3 | Biome constants, info tables, derived lookup arrays and color table; data-driven. |
| `js/charts.js` | 3 | Canvas 2D sparkline, population and species chart helpers. |
| `js/icons.js` | 3 | Icon part tables (150 icons including the map-only `egg` and the weather/UI set `cloud`, `rain`, `snow`, `drop`, `flag`; 12-column atlas) plus SVG and premultiplied texture-atlas builders. Long but flat; new icons are appended and are mostly hand-drawn path data, with a few small shape helpers (`ell`, `ellR`, `spokes`, `starPath`, `ringOf`). Design work needs a visual gallery check. |
| `js/mapGenerator.js` | 6.5 | WorldMap: noise terrain, edge falloff, altitude curve, climate and biome derivation; priority-flood hydrology (depression lakes, flow-accumulation rivers with meander, bank shaping). Layers interact. |
| `js/render.js` | 7.5 | WebGL2 terrain and instanced-sprite shaders, textures, camera; GL state and shader coupling need care. Data views are table-driven (`RAMPS`, `LIVE_MODES`); sprite layers (plants, bug swarm particles, animals) are separate `_push*` passes. Disease adds a live view, blight and infection tints through reused scratch buffers, and per-animal virus markers and strain rings. A fourth texture (bug clouds) with noise-shaped shader haze, a zoom-gated domain warp, depth and shore shading, and animal shadows add zoom-dependent crossfades between cloud and particle swarms. Part 3 slice 3 made the Moisture view live (weather wetness plus a snow tint) and added a categorical Territory view that blends owner species colors from the species lookup over the biome base. Part 3b slice P scales seedling sprites by stage and greys old plants in `_tinted`. Part 3b slice 3 made Territory per-animal with saturated 80% fills, dark 4-neighbour outlines and a 10-tick fade (flat shader: no snow, warp or clouds), scales animal sprites, dots, shadows and rings by the growth factor `gf`, fades elders, and adds an `_pushEggs` pass (zoom ≥ 3) with a pale species tint. Part 4 slice 1 adds a second shader program for the storm overlay (one noise-cloud quad per storm, screen-space rain and snow, zoom-faded, skipped in flat views), live Rainfall and Water/Thirst ramp views, and thirst markers on animals in the water view. |
| `js/main.js` | 6.5 | App glue: UI wiring, panels, `app` state, frame loop; wide but mostly orchestration. Species groups (plant, animal, bug, pathogen) are routed by `group` checks and `TAB_GROUP` spread over tab, detail, tooltip and click code; pathogens add a hosts section and cross-links to host species. Theme cycling, collapsible cards with stored state, and SVG row sparklines add localStorage-backed UI state. Raised from 6 in Part 4 slice 2: a tree/help overlay with its own open state and Escape ordering, keyboard shortcuts, the weather badge, five extra stat cards with custom sub-lines, and per-animal life-stage passes for the detail and tooltip. |
| `js/tree.js` | 5.5 | `FamilyTree`: builds a species or whole-kingdom lineage from `parentId`/`children` (hidden-parent resolution, iterative DFS ordering) and draws it as a canvas timeline with year grid, connectors, extinct styling, label placement fallbacks, a hover tooltip, drag-pan with click-to-select and wheel zoom on both axes. Self-contained, but the layout and hit-test maths must stay consistent between draw and input. |
| `js/save.js` | 5.5 | `EvoSave`: generic identity-preserving graph serializer for the whole `Ecosystem` (BFS ids, `$r`/`$w`/`$wa` references, typed arrays in an aligned binary section, Maps, Sets and class prototypes from a fixed class table) inside a gzip container with a JSON header, and a two-pass loader. No per-field skip lists, so new sim state is saved automatically; a new class must be added to `classTable()`, and state that is not plain data (functions, DOM objects) makes saving throw. The world is regenerated from seed, so any future sim code that mutates `WorldMap` layers would need those layers saved. |
| `js/sim/core.js` | 5.5 | FastRng, fitness, mutation and distance helpers, plus the species registry (sibling-aware `matchDaughter`, `canSplit`, `merge`, `speciations`); small but underpins all evolution and population bookkeeping. |
| `js/sim/soil.js` | 4.5 | SoilLayer: per-tile nutrient store, separable 3x3 demand pooling, satisfaction, a litter pool that decays into nutrients, litter consumption by detritivores, and carcass deposits split into a carrion store that rots into litter and is eaten by scavengers. Small, but tightly coupled to plant and fungus balance. |
| `js/sim/plants.js` | 8 | Plant genes (15, including pest defence and blight resistance), archetypes, speciation, dispersal and logistic growth over two slot planes (canopy/understory), with light, health and soil coupling. Also fruit stocks and seasons, flowers and the bloom spread bonus, and decomposer and mycorrhizal fungi with litter-limited K. Pest damage to health and biomass, and bug pollination feeding fruit and bloom spread. Blight slot arrays (`blight`, `blightT`, `blightImm`) and blight deaths in the health branch. Shared plantSeed competition and a typed-array layout, plus ecology tuning. Icon variants per category (`plantIcon`). Part 3b slice P adds per-slot age and lifespan, seedling/mature/old stages (seedling K ramp, no floor, grazing kills; old growth, fruit and death), and a per-tile seed bank fed by failed spreads and drained by germination, which couples `plantSeed`, `_assign` and `graze` to the stage arrays. Part 3b slice 2 makes old-age deaths self-seed their tile's bank (`_selfSeed`) and spares water seedlings from grazing. |
| `js/sim/animals.js` | 9 | Animal genome, spatial grid, diet, predation, energy, reproduction and speciation. Also fruit eating and seed carriage, three toxin types (mild, confusion, lethal), and species-level aversion learning with hue mimicry. Small omnivores eat bugs (carnivores excluded), and parasites drain energy with host-size matching. Disease resistance gene and per-animal infection fields: sickness cost, slowdown and death, recovery with immunity, own-cell contact spread, predation, carcass and vector exposure, and inherited innate immunity. Scavenging gene (index 10) with carrion efficiency, a carrion forage lure and eat block, and a hunting penalty; the `carrion` category and the role-tagged carrion-eater archetype. Every `AG`-strided loop must follow a gene-count change. Part 3 slice 2 added genes 11–14 (territory, herding, cold-bloodedness, drought tolerance), the amphibious domain 2 with its walk bit, thirst with water-gradient seeking and dehydration deaths, per-tile territory ownership with rival chasing, herd cohesion and safety, cold-blooded metabolism and slowdown, carrion-cell seeking, and the inherited resistance nudge. Part 3b slice 1 added life stages: growth factor `gf` (juveniles scale bite, intake, `emax`, metabolism and predation mass) and elder factor `ef` (speed, bite, fertility cut-off, infection), a hazard-curve old-age death, a `parent` field with juvenile parent-following, egg laying in `_reproduce` for water animals, amphibians and reptiles (with `_eggTile` and per-clutch daughter reuse), and opportunistic egg eating. Part 3b slice 2 added the balance terms: a generalist tax on plant and meat energy by diet (`_genT`), cold-blooded upkeep, a drought-tolerance metabolism cost, a land-scavenger plant penalty, herbivore graze efficiency, an egg lure in `_pickForage`, and water herbivores eating eggs. The most interdependent logic. |
| `js/sim/bugs.js` | 6 | BugLayer: four density planes (pests, detritivores, parasites, pollinators) with per-cell genomes, cached fit/rate, logistic growth, spread with masked mutation and speciation, locust swarms, pollinator collapse detection and reintroduction, and per-category icon variants (`bugIcon`). Couples plants, soil and animals; perf-sensitive inlined loops. |
| `js/sim/disease.js` | 6 | DiseaseLayer: pathogen strains as registry species (population = infected hosts), strain genes with a virulence/transmission trade-off, host-range compatibility and host jumps, strain mutation and speciation, per-tile carcass and vector exposure planes, plant blight list with neighbour spread and monoculture boost, emergence, die-off detection and a clear-all toggle. Strains take icons from fixed lists by id. Registry bookkeeping must stay exactly in sync with the host arrays. |
| `js/sim/weather.js` | 5 | WeatherLayer: per-tile wetness and snow with evaporation and melt, a fixed pool of moving storms that rain or snow on noisy discs by effective temperature, yearly droughts that dry low-flow streams and ponds, a drinkable `fresh` mask, a BFS distance-to-fresh-water field rebuilt on drought changes, and the `moistMul` plant growth multiplier. Tuned so mean `moistMul` stays near 1; the constants interact (spawn rate, rain, evaporation). |
| `js/sim/eggs.js` | 4.5 | EggPool: structure-of-arrays egg store (position, tile, species, genome, energy, float hatch timer, parent domain, innate immunity) with a per-tile head/next list for eaters, cold-slowed reptile incubation, snow and drying failures plus a base per-tick failure (slice 2), hatching through `animals.spawn` (registry population added only at hatch), compaction that rebuilds the tile lists, and species remap for merges. Small, but its counts and species ids must stay consistent with the animal pool and registry. |
| `js/sim/ecosystem.js` | 6.5 | Ties plants, weather, bugs, animals and disease together (weather steps after plants, with the `weather` option and `stats.weather`/`meanWet`) with nine stat groups (land scavengers are counted before the diet split), bug, disease and carrion stats (including the rolling disease share and outbreak tracking in `_diseaseStats`), migrations (including role-tagged scavenger and water-omnivore revival and bug reintroduction; predator and scavenger revival count omnivores as prey), disease emergence every 60 ticks, the disease toggle and history sampling. Part 3 slice 2 raised the groups to nine (amphibians by domain 2 and reptiles by `cold > 0.5` are classified first), wires `animals.setWeather` before the founders, adds amphibian and reptile revival (slice 3 also revives water herbivores whenever they hit 0), and the thirst, herd and territory stats (`_thirstStats`, `_herdStats`). Part 3b slice 2 changes herbivore revival to fire on land herbivores alone below `HERB_RESCUE`. Part 3b slice 1 builds the `EggPool`, steps it after the animals, adds `stats.stages` and `stats.eggs`, and remaps egg species in `_mergePass`.
