Workshop

Turn ingots and planks into permanent research. All 42 levels survive rebirth. Research buys one level at a time, regardless of the Buy setting.

Required expansion chain

Workshop automatically begins Mining → Mastery → Rebirth → Smeltery → Forest → Sawmill → Workshop. Finish each expansion to unlock the next. Smeltery expansion opens Forest; Forest expansion builds Sawmill. There is no additional unlock purchase between expansions.

Each expansion has five production milestones targeting about 20 minutes of development, budgeted for current production and upgrade growth. Only fresh production counts; stronger production can finish sooner. All progress and features survive rebirth. Targets stay fixed, including the starting ore for Mastery. No clock locks.
