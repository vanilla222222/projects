Research

Research is permanent and works on every map. Earn Moonstones by starring up maps and by clearing boss waves on starred maps for the first time. On starred maps each Pony or Ability research level also makes DNBs tougher. Economy and Utility research never does.

Heroes

One hero per map. Heroes cannot fall, level up from kills on this map and keep their choice when you star up. You can change hero between waves.

Welcome back

Upgrade rules

    Add a rule

    Rules are checked top to bottom for every pony. Each one buys when it is affordable after the reserve, then the next check starts again from the top. A pony with its own rules ignores its race rules.

    Board plans

    A plan stores every pony on this map with its race, spot, paths, training levels and target mode, plus the hero spot. Restoring buys what you can afford in order and queues the rest as cash comes in.

    Challenges

    Challenge runs are played on a fresh sandbox board with their own cash and lives. Your maps, ponies and waves are never touched. Research, achievements and your hero come along. Lives carry over from wave to wave, and a run ends when you win, lose every life or quit.
