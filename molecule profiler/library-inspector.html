Calculating basic field checks…

  • DrugCentral named compounds: names and structures from the University of New Mexico’s DrugCentral download, snapshot 2021-09-01, under CC BY-SA 4.0. Structures were selected, checked and reserialized; formulas were recalculated. Source links, CAS identifiers and source IDs are searchable. This is a historical structure collection, not a current approval list. Import methods and attribution.
  • ChEBI curated compounds: named records from the curated ChEBI three-star collection, snapshot 2026-10-06, licensed CC BY 4.0. Source names, identifiers and structure identity are checked during import. Batch source and validation details.
  • Other named/reference entries: not an independently verified or curated set. The class label does not establish a chemical identity or activity claim.
  • ESOL-labelled entries: labelled as a solubility-reference subset; matching them to measured values and authoritative records requires independent validation.
  • Ester/ether/ketone series: structural series examples, not measured biological reference annotations.
  • Fragment examples: small structural examples rather than pharmacological reference data.

Names are sourced separately from stable reference identifiers. Each molecule has at least ten descriptive tags, including calculated graph features and qualified source labels. Click any tag to filter, or type a tag above. Original class labels remain prefixed with “Label:” and do not establish biological activity. Stored-graph charge and descriptor bins are not physiological or clinical predictions. Name and tag methods. The application includes an independent RDKit audit script, but its presence does not mean the stored data have all been verified.
