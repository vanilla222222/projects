Structure search

Search the stored graphs by identity, concrete-SMILES substructure or project ECFP4 fingerprint similarity. Identity preserves charge, isotope and hydrogen counts; stereo can be required or ignored. Substructure matching is topological and does not transfer pharmacology.

Draw a connectivity query

Click empty space to add an atom and bond it to the selected atom. Click atoms to connect them. Deselect to start a separate fragment. This sketcher records connectivity only; use SMILES for charge, isotope and stereo specifications.

Paste a structure or draw a query. Search scans the complete composed library; at most 100 hits are displayed.

Family SAR explorer

Align measured references around a concrete shared core and inspect assay-specific potency and efficacy. Source classifications and censored results are not plotted as exact measurements. Shared structure does not establish a causal substituent effect.

The default core selects the measured 2C series. Choose another core and family to explore other references.

Matched molecular pairs

Explore reviewed structures related by one non-ring single-bond fragment substitution, including hydrogen replacements. The common core contains at least six heavy atoms and half of each parent; variable fragments contain at most eight heavy atoms. Only compatible exact measurements support endpoint differences.

The threshold flags candidates for review, not statistically established activity cliffs.

Probe and control finder

Find explicitly documented chemical probes by source-reported target and assay. Controls retain their own identity and assay scope. Related domain/family controls are distinguished from exact assay-matched controls.

Current probe/control records cover BET bromodomains and JMJD3/KDM6B. Missing counter-screen data are shown explicitly.

Claim-to-experiment tracing

Follow a reported action, endpoint, control or relationship from the stored graph to its identity witnesses and source observations. Database classifications, measured results and imported binding evidence retain separate labels; supporting rows may refer to the same publication.

Use “Trace claims” in a dossier or choose a compound here.

Evidence update tracking

Compare pinned public releases of the curated overlay. Inspect new or removed references, identity fields, measured values and assay context, action classifications, source witnesses and review decisions. A source classification is not a new measurement, and a removed record is not necessarily a retraction.

Snapshots retain source hashes and public commit references. New derived tools do not count as new experimental evidence.

Assay-aware comparison

Choose up to six compounds. Values are grouped by their reported target, species, assay, endpoint, tested form and source. Separate groups are not a potency ranking.

Family evidence matrix

Each cell describes imported evidence for the stored structure and selected target. No imported evidence means unknown; a low activity bound or control designation applies only to its source assay.

Choose a family, then show its matrix.

Conflict review centre

Inspect identity disagreements and differing action reports. Assay-dependent differences are review candidates; they are not automatically erroneous. Evidence guards and scoped selections do not resolve an underlying disagreement.

Load the current review candidates.

Coverage-driven curation queue

Workflow order: identity review → action review → functional evidence → assay controls → scaffold coverage. These are transparent planning rules, not scientific confidence scores. Gaps describe this index and do not establish that an experiment has never been done.

Calculating basic field checks…

  • DrugCentral named compounds: names and structures from the University of New Mexico’s DrugCentral download, snapshot 2021-09-01, under CC BY-SA 4.0. Structures were selected, checked and reserialized; formulas were recalculated. Source links, CAS identifiers and source IDs are searchable. This is a historical structure collection, not a current approval list. Import methods and attribution.
  • ChEBI curated compounds: named records from the curated ChEBI three-star collection, snapshot 2026-10-06, licensed CC BY 4.0. Source names, identifiers and structure identity are checked during import. Batch source and validation details.
  • Other named/reference entries: not an independently verified or curated set. The class label does not establish a chemical identity or activity claim.
  • ESOL-labelled entries: labelled as a solubility-reference subset; matching them to measured values and authoritative records requires independent validation.
  • Ester/ether/ketone series: structural series examples, not measured biological reference annotations.
  • Fragment examples: small structural examples rather than pharmacological reference data.

Names are sourced separately from stable reference identifiers. Each molecule has at least ten descriptive tags, including calculated graph features and qualified source labels. Click any tag to filter, or type a tag above. Original class labels remain prefixed with “Label:” and do not establish biological activity. Stored-graph charge and descriptor bins are not physiological or clinical predictions. Name and tag methods. The application includes an independent RDKit audit script, but its presence does not mean the stored data have all been verified.
