What this is

Everything here runs in the page: the SMILES parser, ring perception, aromaticity, kekulization and hydrogen counting are implemented from scratch, and the descriptors follow their published definitions — Ertl's topological polar surface area, Wildman–Crippen log P and molar refractivity, Lipschitz-style hydrogen-bond counts, and the strict rotatable-bond definition. The descriptor layer is checked against RDKit on a 40-molecule reference set; TPSA, log P, molar refractivity, rotatable bonds, the H-bond counts and the sp³ fraction all agree exactly. The fingerprints are checked the same way: on the 80 reference drugs below, every bit-count and all 3160 pairwise similarity scores match RDKit exactly for each of the four methods.

The filters are the published ones (Lipinski, Ghose, Veber, Egan, Muegge, lead-likeness) and the bioavailability score follows Martin's tiers. Where a service like SwissADME uses a trained model that was never released — XLOGP3, its solubility models, its pharmacokinetic classifiers — this tool does not pretend to reproduce it; it uses an open method and says which one.
