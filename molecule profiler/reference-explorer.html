Evidence and provenance

Reference entries are unverified unless a specific authoritative record is cited. Descriptive signalling relationships are context, not binding measurements. Chemical similarity is not proof of shared biological activity.

Molecule PubChem and IUPHAR lookup links initiate searches and are not verified interaction records. Receptor cards link separately to record-level NCBI Gene sources and additional mechanism sources. Those identity/function sources do not validate every possible clinical consequence; the summaries are educational and not a systematic clinical evidence review.
